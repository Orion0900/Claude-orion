# Browser checks

A script that drives the built app in headless Chromium on an iPhone-sized,
touch-enabled screen. It serves `dist/` and `dist-artifact/` itself.

```bash
npm run build
npm run artifact
node tests/smoke.mjs
```

It opens on the budget as written down and checks every number: $3,900 twice
a month making $7,800, $3,900 Investing and $3,053 Living with $847 left, the
three rules and each paycheck's rows down to $423 in checking. Then it takes
a raise, tries every 2 weeks and once a month, ticks off a payday, checks the
Amazon card before it's typed in, then under and over its $1,000, with today's
pace on its mark, finds markets, self investment, fixed
and variable folded to their totals and opens them one at a time, renames a
card, changes, removes and restores a subscription and adds one, breaks Car
down without its total moving, adds a variable category (and closes a blank
one), moves one to self investment and back, deletes one and undoes it, runs
the projection, fills in gross pay, bonus and the 401k, and reloads to find
it all still there, with the halves folded again, and the card's past months
listed with each one over or under.

With a stand-in clock it then opens the app a minute before midnight on the
14th, ticks a check and types the card, and lets midnight pass: the
checklist starts over for the 15th with the tick in History. Brought back on
December 1st after weeks in the background, the card starts a new month and
November is under Past months.

Then the artifact build, with a stand-in for the artifact's database: the
loading screen, the shared budget arriving, an edit written back, another
device's change showing up, a budget saved by the first version opening
on the card system and saving in the new shape, an empty database opening an empty
budget, and no database at all. It fails on any page or console error. Set
`SHOTS=<dir>` to save screenshots along the way.

`node tests/look.mjs <dir>` saves screenshots of the phone in both themes,
the subscriptions popout and a wide screen.
